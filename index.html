The actual business model

From Alcare stock to reseller

The reseller buys stock from Alcare, receives it, and then manages their own retail customers independently.

1

Agent orders stock

Agent signs in, browses Alcare’s current catalogue, selects quantities and reviews the stock order.

2

Pay via PayFast

The agent pays securely. A successful PayFast confirmation updates the order automatically.

3

Alcare fulfils

The office sees the paid order, picks stock, packs it and moves it through clear fulfilment statuses.

4

Ship to agent

Alcare records courier and tracking details where useful. Courier booking itself stays outside V1.

5

Agent sells

The reseller receives stock and handles their own end-customer sales. They return to Alcare when they need to reorder.

Agent experience

Ordering stock should take minutes

The mobile experience is deliberately simple. An agent sees only the current Alcare catalogue, their pricing, their cart, payment and their own order history.

  • Search and filter the product catalogue
  • Select quantities and see a running order total
  • Use the agent’s saved delivery address
  • Pay from the order using PayFast
  • See paid, processing, dispatched and completed orders
  • Reorder without recapturing customer information
Supplier control

Alcare owns the catalogue

Routine catalogue changes belong in the office dashboard, not in a developer’s task list. Alcare can keep the ordering app current themselves.

  • Add and edit products
  • Maintain reseller pricing
  • Manage categories and product availability
  • Activate or deactivate products
  • Update catalogue information without redeploying the app
  • Historic orders retain the price used at the time of order
Payment automation

PayFast becomes part of the order flow

The normal successful order should not require somebody in the office to open PayFast, find the transaction and manually mark an order paid.

Sales intelligence

Useful information from data already being captured

No extra paperwork. The ordering system naturally creates a useful picture of reseller activity and product movement.

Agent performance

Sales value, order count, last order date and activity by reseller.

Product performance

Top products by revenue and units, category performance and movement over time.

Inactive agents

Flag previously active resellers who have not placed a stock order for a selected period.

Operational exceptions

Paid orders still waiting for processing, dispatch or completion are easy to spot.

Phase 1 scope

Focused on removing administration

Courier API integration stays out. The important V1 is the reseller order, payment and office workflow.

Included

  • Branded responsive agent ordering app
  • Agent accounts and saved delivery details
  • Supplier-updatable product catalogue
  • Reseller pricing and quantity ordering
  • PayFast payment integration
  • Automatic payment-status update
  • Office paid-order processing queue
  • Pick, pack and fulfilment statuses
  • Manual courier / tracking fields
  • Agent order history and status visibility
  • Sales and reseller intelligence
  • Data export and audit history

Not required for V1

  • Courier API integration
  • End-customer CRM for reseller customers
  • Accounting package integration
  • Warehouse or ERP stock synchronisation
  • Native iPhone / Android applications
  • Advanced commission or payroll processing
  • Predictive AI or complex BI tooling

The reseller sells. Alcare fulfils. The system removes the paperwork between them.

The prototype now reflects the actual Alcare reseller model and can be used to validate the operating flow before the live backend and PayFast merchant account are connected.
