The proposed flow

Simple from order to delivery

The first phase focuses on removing paper, reducing recapturing and giving the office a clean view of every order.

Capture on mobile

Agents select the customer, products and quantities directly from their phone.

Process in the office

Orders arrive complete, legible and ready to review without spreadsheet recapturing.

Prepare for courier

Delivery information is captured upfront, making packing and dispatch easier.

Agent experience

Fast product ordering from the field

A clear catalogue lets agents find products, enter quantities and build an order without relying on printed price lists or handwritten forms.

  • Searchable product catalogue
  • Product pricing and quantity selection
  • Clean mobile interface for 10–100 agents
  • Final product list and prices supplied by Alcare
Complete information

Orders arrive ready to process

Customer, product and delivery information can be submitted together, reducing follow-ups and errors before an order reaches the packing team.

  • Customer and contact details
  • Delivery address and method
  • Order totals and internal notes
  • Immediate digital submission to the office
