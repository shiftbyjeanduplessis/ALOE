Keep it simple and consistent
WARM-UP SETS & REPS
5 minutes on a treadmill, bike or cross trainer. 3 sets of 12 unless the workout says otherwise.
REST CHOOSE THE RIGHT WEIGHT
60-75 seconds between working sets. The final 2-3 reps should feel challenging, but your form
must stay good.
GOOD FORM LISTEN TO YOUR BODY
Move with control. Use a range of movement you can own. Muscle effort is expected. Sharp pain is not.
WALKING MISSED SESSION
Add two 30-40 minute walks each week where possible. Do not double up hard sessions to catch up. Continue with
the next sensible workout.
The program should feel challenging, not chaotic. Focus on controlled reps, steady progress and showing up for the next
session.
