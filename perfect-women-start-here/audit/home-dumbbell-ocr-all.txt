Get alittle stronger over time

Progressive overload means gradually asking your muscles to do slightly more. In this program, the simplest method is to build clean
reps frst, then use the next sensible dumbbell weight.
@ START COMFORTABLE

Choose a load thatlets you reach the bottom of the rep range with control. The final 2-3 reps should feel challenging.
@ OWN THE TOP OF THE RANGE

Stay at the same weightuntil every setreaches the top of the rep range cleanly.
@ INCREASE SLIGHTLY

Move to the next sensible dumbbell weightor the smallest available increase.
@ BUILD BACK UP

After increasing, reps may drop. Keep the new weightand build back toward the top of the range.
SIMPLE DECISION RULE
Can't reach the minimum reps cleanly Keep the same weight or reduce slightly.
Top reps are hard but clean Perfect. Stay at this weight
Top reps feel comfortable Increase slightly next time.
LOG YOUR WEIGHTS
If you do notrecord the weight used, it is easy to repeat the same workout without actually progressing.
