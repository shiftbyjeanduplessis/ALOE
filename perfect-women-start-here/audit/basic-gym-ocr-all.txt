PROGR OVERLOAD
Get a little stronger over time
Progressive overload means gradually asking your muscles to do slightly more. In this program the main method is simple: master 3 x 12
with good form, then increase the weight by the smallest sensible amount.
eo START AT A COMFORTABLE WEIGHT
Choose a load that allows 12 good reps. The last 2-3 reps should feel challenging.
@ OWN3X12
Stay at that weight until all three sets of 12 are clean and controlled.
3) INCREASE SLIGHTLY
Move to the next sensible dumbbell or machine increment
C4] BUILD BACK TO 12
After increasing, 8-10 good reps is fne. Keep the new weight and build back toward 12
SIMPLE DECISION RULE
Can't reach 12 cleanly Keep the same weight or reduce slightly.
3x 12 is hard but clean Perfect. Stay at this weight.
3x 12 feels comfortable Increase the weight slightly next time.
LOG YOUR WEIGHTS
If you do not record the weights, it is easy to repeat the same workout for eight weeks without actually progressing.

Keep it simple and consistent
WARM-UP SETS & REPS
5 minutes on a treadmill, bike or cross trainer. 3 sets of 12 unless the workout says otherwise.
REST CHOOSE THE RIGHT WEIGHT
60-75 seconds between working sets. The final 2-3 reps should feel challenging, but your form
must stay good.
GOOD FORM LISTEN TO YOUR BODY
Move with control. Use a range of movement you can own. Muscle effort is expected. Sharp pain is not.
WALKING MISSED SESSION
Add two 30-40 minute walks each week where possible. Do not double up hard sessions to catch up. Continue with
the next sensible workout.
The program should feel challenging, not chaotic. Focus on controlled reps, steady progress and showing up for the next
session.
